# Acme Pay customer support playbook

For everyone in Customer Support. Our merchants trust us with their money; every rule here exists because getting it
wrong has cost a merchant money before.

## Identity checks

Before discussing an account, pass three checks: the merchant's date of birth, the business postcode, and the amount
of their last payout. Never ask for a full card number, a password or a one-time passcode. If a caller fails a
check, don't give hints; offer to call them back on the number on file.

## Refunds

- Agents can approve refunds up to £250 on their own.
- Refunds over £250 need team-lead approval in the Refunds queue before you promise anything to the merchant.
- Refunds always go back to the original payment method. Never refund to a different card or bank account.
- Don't promise a refund date until it has been approved.

## Bank detail changes

- Never change payout bank details because of an email or chat message, even if it comes from the merchant's
  registered email address.
- Bank details can only change after a **callback to the phone number on file** and approval in the merchant's app
  with two-factor authentication.
- Every change puts a 24-hour hold on payouts. Never remove the hold because the merchant is in a hurry.
- Pressure to rush a bank change ("we need it today", "the owner is travelling") is a fraud signal. Flag the account
  to the fraud team.

## Vulnerable customers

Signs include bereavement, serious illness, money worries or confusion. Slow down and listen. Offer to pause payment
collections for 30 days. With the customer's consent, add a vulnerability flag so nobody makes them repeat their
story. Never pressure a vulnerable customer into a decision on the call.

## Complaints

- Acknowledge every complaint within 24 hours.
- Send a final response within 8 weeks. This is a regulatory deadline (FCA DISP rules).
- If a merchant mentions the Financial Ombudsman, escalate to the complaints team lead the same day.
- Log every complaint in the Complaints register, even if you resolved it on the call.

## Chargebacks

When a cardholder disputes a payment with their bank, the merchant receives a chargeback. Explain the process
plainly: the merchant has 7 days to upload evidence (receipt, delivery proof) in their dashboard. Never promise a
merchant they will win a chargeback, and never advise them to refund the cardholder directly while a dispute is open,
because they could lose the money twice.

## Third parties on the account

Only people listed as account users can discuss the account. If an accountant, spouse or "business partner" calls,
politely explain you can only speak to listed users, and that the owner can add them in the app. Never confirm
whether a business is an Acme Pay customer to someone who isn't a listed user.

## Tone of voice

Write the way you'd talk to a busy shop owner: short sentences, no jargon, and always say what happens next and when.
If you don't know, say so and give a time you'll come back by.
