# Acme Pay security and compliance policy

Every Acme Pay employee completes this training when they join and again every year. It supports our SOC 2 and
ISO 27001 commitments.

## Suspicious emails and phishing

- Never click links or open attachments in an email you weren't expecting. Hover to check the real address first.
- Report any suspicious email with the **Report Phish** button in your mail client. Don't forward it to colleagues.
- Urgency is the biggest warning sign: "today", "immediately", "or your account will be closed".
- Any request to pay an invoice, change bank details or share a password must be verified through a channel you
  already know (the phone number on file, or a colleague in person). Never verify using contact details from the
  email itself.
- If you clicked a link or entered your password, report it in #security-incidents straight away. Speed matters
  more than embarrassment.

## Data protection

- Customer data lives in Vault. Never paste customer names, account numbers or card details into Slack, email or AI
  tools.
- Never confirm to a third party whether someone is an Acme Pay customer.
- Data subject access requests (a customer asking for their data) go to privacy@acmepay.example within 24 hours.
  Don't answer them yourself.
- Lost laptop or phone: report it in #security-incidents within 1 hour so it can be wiped.

## Gifts and hospitality

- You may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to £50. Record every one in the gift register within 7 days.
- Anything over £50: politely decline, or ask Compliance before accepting.
- Never accept anything from a supplier during an active tender or contract renewal, whatever its value.
- Cash, vouchers and gift cards are never accepted.

## Office access and visitors

- Badge in every time, even if someone holds the door. Don't let anyone follow you through a secure door.
- If someone without a visible badge tries to follow you in, politely ask to see their badge or direct them to
  reception. Don't let them in to be helpful.
- Visitors sign in at reception and are escorted at all times.

## Reporting incidents

Report suspected security incidents in #security-incidents within 1 hour: a phishing click, a lost device, data sent
to the wrong person, or a stranger in the office. Don't investigate on your own and don't delete evidence. The
security team will take it from there.

## AI tools

- Only use AI tools on the approved list in the IT portal. Never paste customer data, card details, account numbers
  or internal code into a tool that isn't on the list.
- Anonymise before you ask: "a merchant in Leeds disputes a £300 refund" is fine; their name and account number are
  not.
- You are responsible for anything an AI tool writes for you. Check facts against our policies before sending it to a
  customer.

## Passwords and multi-factor authentication

- Use the company password manager. Never reuse your Acme Pay password anywhere else.
- Never approve a multi-factor prompt you didn't start. Unexpected prompts mean someone has your password: report it
  in #security-incidents.
- IT will never ask for your password or a one-time code, by phone, chat or email.

## Clean desk and screens

- Lock your screen whenever you step away (Windows key + L).
- Don't leave printed customer information on your desk. Use the confidential shredding bins.
- On calls in public places, don't read out customer details where others can hear.
