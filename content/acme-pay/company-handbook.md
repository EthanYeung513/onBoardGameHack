# Acme Pay company handbook

Acme Pay is a fictional payments company used for the GymQuest demo. None of this describes a real company.

## Who we are

Acme Pay moves money for 40,000 small businesses across the UK and EU. We take card payments for merchants and pay
the money out to their bank accounts every working day. About 200 people work here across Customer Support,
Operations, Engineering, Sales and Compliance. Our values are: protect the customer's money, say what you don't know,
and write it down.

## Who owns what

- **Customer Support** owns every conversation with merchants: chat, phone and email. Team leads approve exceptions.
- **Payments Engineering** owns the Ledger, the payout service and the payment gateway.
- **Compliance** owns the security policy, data protection, gifts and anti-money-laundering checks.
- **Operations** owns the Manchester warehouse that ships card terminals to merchants.
- **People team** owns onboarding, expenses and the handbook itself.

If you don't know who owns something, ask in #who-owns-what. Never guess and act on behalf of another team.

## Expenses

- Meals while travelling for work: up to £30 per day, with an itemised receipt.
- Submit receipts in the Expenses app within 14 days. Claims older than 90 days are not paid.
- Alcohol is never expensed, except at team events approved in advance by a director.
- Train is the default for UK travel under 4 hours. Flights need your manager's approval before booking.
- Never split one purchase across several claims to stay under a limit.

## Escalation

When something goes wrong, escalate early. Tell your team lead first. If a customer's money is at risk, or you think
there has been a security incident, you can go straight to #security-incidents without waiting for your lead.
Nobody is ever blamed for escalating something that turns out to be fine.
