2. Accounts and passwords

· Use multi-factor authentication on every work account. Use a hardware key or an authenticator app; SMS
    codes are not allowed.

· Store every work password in the company 1Password vault. Never reuse a password.
· Never share your account or MFA codes with anyone, including IT. IT will never ask for them.

3. Devices

· Disk encryption must stay on. Your screen auto-locks after 5 minutes.
· Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk.
· Install operating system updates within 7 days of release.
· Do not access Confidential or Restricted data from personal devices.

4. Phishing

Most attacks start with a message. Treat any message that pushes you to act fast, outside the usual process,
as suspicious.

Warning signs

· Urgency or pressure: "the CEO needs this in 10 minutes".
· A sender domain that is close to ours but not ours, such as mubit-ai.co or rnubit.ai.
· Unexpected attachments, QR codes, or links to log-in pages.

 What to do

 3 If you already clicked or entered credentials, report it in #security-incidents within 15 minutes and change

 Payment and bank detail changes

 Verify every request to change bank details or make an urgent payment by calling the person on a number
 you already have. Never use a phone number from the message itself.

 5. Physical security

 · Badge in every time you enter. Do not hold the door for people you do not recognise: this is tailgating.
 · If someone tries to follow you in, politely ask who they are visiting and send them to reception.
 · Visitors sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge and are escorted at all times.
 · Clear your desk at the end of the day. Confidential papers go in the locked shredding bin.

 6. Production and customer data access

 · Access follows least privilege. Production access is just-in-time, approved by the on-call lead, logged, and
     expires after 4 hours.

 · Never copy customer data into development or test environments. Use synthetic fixtures.

 7. Reporting incidents

 Report any suspected incident, such as a lost laptop, a leaked secret or data sent to the wrong person, within
 1 hour in #security-incidents or to security@mubit.example. When in doubt, report it.
