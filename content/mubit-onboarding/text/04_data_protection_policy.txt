1. What counts as personal data

Personal data is any information that identifies a living person, directly or indirectly: a name, email address,
IP address, device ID, or a customer's end user mentioned inside a trace.

Special category data, such as health, ethnicity, religion or sexual orientation, needs extra protection. If you
find it in a trace or a support ticket, do not copy it anywhere and tell the privacy team.

3. Principles for everyday work

· Use personal data only for the purpose it was collected for.
· Collect the minimum you need. Do not keep extra copies in spreadsheets, notes or Slack.
· Customer traces are kept for 90 days by default unless the customer's contract says otherwise.
· Personal data may only leave the UK or EU through systems approved by the privacy team.

4. Data subject requests

A data subject request is anyone asking to see, correct or delete their data, for example "what do you hold on
me?" or "delete my account".
1 Do not reply to it yourself, even to say "we'll get back to you".
2 Forward it to privacy@mubit.example within 1 working day.
3 The privacy team responds. The legal deadline is one month from receipt.

5. Personal data breaches

A breach is any loss, leak or unauthorised access to personal data, including an email sent to the wrong
person.
