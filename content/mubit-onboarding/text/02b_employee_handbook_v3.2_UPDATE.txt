1. Working hours and location

· Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 UK time. Outside core hours, work when suits you and your team.
· We are hybrid. Tuesday and Thursday are office anchor days at the London office; other days are flexible.
· You may work from abroad for up to 20 working days per year with written approval from the People team,

    requested at least 2 weeks in advance.

2. Probation

Probation lasts 3 months. Your manager holds review conversations at week 6 and week 12. During
probation the notice period is 1 week on either side; after probation it is 1 month.

3. Annual leave

· You get 25 days of annual leave per year plus UK bank holidays.
· The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. You may carry over up to 5 unused days, which must be

    used by 30 June.
· Book leave in PeopleHub at least 2 weeks ahead for anything longer than 3 days. Your manager approves

    it.
· During launch weeks, leave for the teams involved needs approval from the team lead.

4. Sickness absence

· Tell your manager by 09:30 on the first day, by Slack message or phone, and post in #ooo.
· You can self-certify for up to 7 calendar days. After 7 days you need a fit note from a doctor.
· Log every sick day in PeopleHub when you return.

5. Pay and pension

· Salary is paid monthly on the last working day of the month.
· You are auto-enrolled into the company pension. Mubit contributes 5% of qualifying earnings; the default

    employee contribution is 3%.
· Payslips are available in PeopleHub.

7. Equipment

You receive a company laptop on day 1. It stays company property and must be returned within 5 working
days of leaving. Report loss or theft to IT Helpdesk and #security-incidents within 1 hour.

8. Code of conduct

· Treat colleagues, customers and candidates with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination are
    never acceptable.

· Mubit has a duty to take reasonable steps to prevent sexual harassment at work, and every employee is
    expected to help.

· Raise a concern with your manager, the People team, or anonymously through the Speak Up form. The
    People team acknowledges every report within 5 working days.

· Nobody will be treated badly for raising a concern in good faith.

9. Leaving Mubit

Give notice in writing to your manager and the People team. Remaining annual leave is paid out or taken
during notice, agreed with your manager.

10. Using AI tools at work

· Use only the AI tools on the approved list in Notion (Security, then Approved tools).
· Never paste Restricted data, such as customer traces, prompts or credentials, into any AI tool, approved

    or not.
· You are responsible for checking AI-generated work before you ship it or send it to a customer.
