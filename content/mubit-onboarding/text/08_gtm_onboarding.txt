1. Who we sell to

Teams running agents in production that repeat the same kinds of task: extraction, triage, support replies,
review and routing.

Qualifying questions

1 Do you have agents in production today, or only prototypes?
2 Which tasks do your agents run again and again?
3 How do you know when a task succeeded: tests, application checks, or human review?
4 What are your security, data residency and compliance requirements?
A lead without a way to verify outcomes (question 3) is not ready for a pilot. Log it as nurture in the CRM.

2. The pitch in three lines

· Point your model calls at Mubit.
· It calibrates every call at runtime, then builds and refreshes a dedicated model for each task your agents

    repeat, switching over only when it does better.
· Your frontier models stay, and you own what is built.

4. What we never say

Never promise a specific improvement, such as "30% cheaper" or "2x accuracy". Results depend on the workload.
Offer to measure task success, latency and cost per successful task on the customer's own held-out tasks instead.

5. Security questionnaires

· Never fill in a security questionnaire yourself. Post it in #security-questionnaires.
· Standard turnaround is 5 working days. Tell the customer this up front.
· Point the customer to the Trust Center first: it answers most questions.

6. Demos

· Use the demo workspace only. Never show another customer's console, traces or name.
· Never take or share screenshots of a customer's workspace.

8. Pilots

· Pilots last 4 weeks by default.
· Success criteria, measured on held-out tasks, must be agreed in writing before the pilot starts.
· A solutions engineer joins every technical evaluation.

9. CRM hygiene

Log every customer call in the CRM within 24 hours, including next steps and the decision-maker.

10. Launch week talking points

Launch week runs 26 to 30 October 2026, with one major feature released each day. Do not share details of
upcoming features with customers before they are announced.
