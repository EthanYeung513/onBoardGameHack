What Mubit does

Mubit helps teams build reliable agents with a model for every task. Customers point their model calls at
Mubit. Mubit calibrates every call at runtime, then builds and refreshes a dedicated model for each task their
agents repeat, switching over only when the new model does better. Customers keep their frontier models,
and they own what is built.

Our category is agentic runtime calibration: improving how an agent behaves using evidence from its own
runs.

How we measure improvement

We compare a customer's current setup with runtime calibration on held-out tasks, measuring task success,
repeated errors, latency and cost per successful task. We never promise a fixed improvement: results
depend on the workload.

Banyan models and latte/bench

· Banyan is our family of specialised models, built on open-source and open-weight foundations and trained
    through Mubit.

· Banyan Sapling is the first Banyan model. It checks a claim against the policy and evidence provided and
    returns one of: supported, violation, missing information, or inconsistent context, with calibrated
    uncertainty.

Security and trust

Customers send us their agent traces, so trust is part of the product. Mubit is SOC 2 compliant, ISO 27001
certified, ISO 42001 certified, GDPR compliant and HIPAA compliant. Every new joiner completes security
training in their first 14 days (see the Information Security Policy).
