1. Fire safety

· The fire alarm is tested every Wednesday at 11:00. A continuous alarm at any other time means evacuate.
· Leave by the nearest stairs. Never use the lifts.
· Do not stop to collect belongings.
· The assembly point is the plaza in front of the building's main entrance.
· Fire wardens wear orange vests. Follow their instructions and do not go back inside until they say so.
· If you are hosting a visitor, you are responsible for getting them to the assembly point.

2. First aid

The first aid kit and defibrillator are at reception. Trained first aiders are listed on the noticeboard by the
kitchen. In an emergency, call 999 first, then a first aider.

3. Accidents and near misses

Record every accident or near miss in the accident book at reception on the same day, even if nobody was
hurt. A near miss is something that could have caused an injury, such as a trailing cable someone tripped on.

4. Display screen equipment (DSE)

· Complete the DSE self-assessment in PeopleHub within your first 7 days.
· The assessment covers your home set-up as well as the office.
· Ask the People team if you need a different chair, monitor or keyboard.
· Take a short break from the screen at least once an hour.

5. Working from home

· Your home workspace should have a stable desk, a supportive chair and enough light.
· Report any work-related injury at home to the People team the same way as in the office.

6. Wellbeing

Mental health first aiders are listed in #wellbeing. The employee assistance programme offers confidential
support 24 hours a day; details are in PeopleHub.
