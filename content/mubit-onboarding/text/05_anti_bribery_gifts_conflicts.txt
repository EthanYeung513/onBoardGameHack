1. Our rule

We follow the UK Bribery Act 2010. Never offer, give, request or accept anything of value that could
influence, or look like it influences, a business decision. This applies to customers, suppliers, partners and
candidates.

2. Accepting gifts and hospitality

Value                                     What you must do
Up to £50                                 You may accept. No approval needed.
£50 to £150                               Declare it in the gifts register within 5 working days.
Over £150                                 Get approval from the Head of Operations before accepting.
Cash, vouchers or gift cards              Never accept, at any value.
Anything during an active deal or tender  Decline politely and tell your manager.

The gifts register is in Notion under Operations, then Gifts register.

3. Public officials

Get approval from the Head of Operations before offering or accepting anything to or from a public official, at
any value.

4. Never allowed

· Facilitation payments: small payments to speed up a routine process, such as a visa or customs
    clearance.

· Paying a third party to do something we could not do ourselves.
· Splitting a gift into smaller ones to stay under a threshold.

5. Donations and sponsorships

Charitable donations, event sponsorships and conference payments made on behalf of Mubit need approval
from the Head of Operations, whatever the amount.
